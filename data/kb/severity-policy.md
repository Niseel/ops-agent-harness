# Severity policy

## Levels

A service that is down is SEV1.
A degraded service with customer impact is SEV2.
A degraded service without customer impact is SEV3.
A minor issue with a workaround is SEV4.

## When to open an incident

Open an incident when a service is down, or degraded with customer impact. Link the runbook section you used.

## Approval

A person approves every incident the assistant proposes. The person can approve, edit or reject it.
